FAQ

FAQ

How do I extract text from an image for free?
Upload an image, pick a language, click Extract text. OCR runs locally; the result is editable, copyable or downloadable as TXT. Free, no signup.
Are my images uploaded to a server?
No. Recognition runs locally (Tesseract WebAssembly); images are never uploaded — good for sensitive screenshots.
Does it support Chinese? Is it accurate?
Yes — Chinese + English. Clear printed text is accurate; blurry, artistic or handwritten text may have errors you can fix afterward.
Why is the first run slow?
A language pack downloads the first time (the Chinese pack is larger), then caches. Recognition runs locally; large/long text is slower.
